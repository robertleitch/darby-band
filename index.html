📣 When to cheer

Cheer often and loud at contests — other schools' fans may not be cheering for us. Great moments to make noise:

  1. When the band is announced
  2. After the drum majors salute
  3. After the oboe solo, and the big loud note right after it
  4. After every rifle toss and big flag moment
  5. At the end of each section feature or small ensemble (two in Parts 1 and 2, two in Part 3, lots in Part 4)
  6. After the loud section in the middle of Part 2, when the band stands still and does choreography
  7. At the end of each section of the show
  8. When the guard is spinning on the desk props in Part 3
  9. After the bass trombone solo in Part 4
  10. When the band turns around in Part 5
  11. At the end, of course!
